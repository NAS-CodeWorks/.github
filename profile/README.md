# NAS CodeWorks

**Digital Product Studio — Build · Automate · Scale**

NAS CodeWorks builds production-ready software, digital platforms, business systems, AI-enabled workflows, automation, and media technology. We design around real operational needs, with a strong focus on Arabic-first experiences, maintainable architecture, and reliable delivery.

## What we build

- Custom business systems, dashboards, and operational platforms
- Websites and digital products designed around the organization’s identity
- Applied AI and automation integrated into real workflows
- Broadcast, streaming, radio, and metadata systems
- Secure cloud delivery, CI/CD, monitoring, and performance engineering

## Public products

- [Maktoob](https://github.com/NAS-CodeWorks/maktoob) — Arabic-first Windows contract management for Iraqi offices, designed for local-first operation.

## Engineering principles

- Production reliability before demos
- Secure-by-default architecture
- Clear, low-friction operator workflows
- Modular systems that can evolve safely
- Documented and reviewable delivery through GitHub
